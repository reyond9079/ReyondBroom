# Editor sky background

Starting with alpha.114, the cloudless blue background is enabled by default in
all five game profiles: Quake 1, Quake II, Quake III Arena, Half-Life and FTE Custom.

Open **Workspace > Appearance**, or **Edit > Preferences > View**:

- **Show sky background** enables or removes the 3D editor backdrop.
- Click the color swatch beside **Sky color** to open the picker. Changes appear immediately.
- **Reset sky color** restores the original blue gradient without changing visibility.

The horizon and lower hemisphere follow the selected color. Black produces a
black sky, including the sun contribution. Turning the background off removes
its draw pass and reveals the ordinary editor background. Orthographic views,
BSP preview and mod preview retain their existing rendering. Game-profile
material defaults and Shader Pack restrictions are unchanged. An explicit Shader
Pack sky shell retains its procedural atmosphere.

Settings are global across profiles and saved in the existing `appearance.cfg`.
Older appearance files still load; an explicit previously disabled custom-profile
sky remains disabled until the checkbox is enabled. Loading resources or
switching profiles does not reset the selected sky color/visibility. These are
editor preferences: MAPs, BSPs and external mod resources are not rewritten.

## Validation

Windows OpenGL, Linux native and Linux SDL2 under WSL/Mesa passed 31 checks
each: all five profile switches, actual background submission, retaining the
choice through material setup, and color/visibility round trips after restart.
Rendered captures confirmed blue, red, disabled and black states on all three
builds. Additional Windows checks covered orthographic suppression and keeping
the Shader Pack's explicit procedural sky shell. Native Arch/KDE hardware was
not available for this verification.
